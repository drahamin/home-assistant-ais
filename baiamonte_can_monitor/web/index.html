The adapter automatically controls transmit direction. Use the battery’s dedicated RS485 port, not PCS, LINK-0, or LINK-1.

NO-REPLY CHECKLIST

If the adapter connects but a battery is offline

  1. Confirm both batteries are powered and their inter-battery link remains connected.
  2. Confirm the app uses 9600 baud, 8 data bits, no parity, and 1 stop bit.
  3. Verify Battery 1 is address 1 and Battery 2 is address 2.
  4. Verify pin 6 reaches TXD+ and pin 5 reaches TXD−; with power off, swap A/B only if the adapter convention differs.
  5. Never connect battery RJ45 pin 2 (12 V) to the USB adapter.
