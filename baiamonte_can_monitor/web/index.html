ZERO-FRAME CHECKLIST

If the adapter connects but receives nothing

  1. Confirm inverter and battery master are powered and communicating.
  2. Keep the CANable 120Ω switch off.
  3. Verify CAN-H and CAN-L continuity to the exact RJ45 pins.
  4. With equipment powered off, swap H/L at the CANable only and retry if the cable mapping is uncertain.
  5. Keep the configured rate at 500 kbit/s for Growatt BMS CAN v1.04.
